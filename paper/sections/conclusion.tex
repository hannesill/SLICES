\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\SLICES{} provides a fixed sparse-ICU benchmark specification, interchangeable SSL objectives, shared downstream evaluation, and auditable reference results. At full labels, baselines lead half of the cells: GRU-D leads 24-hour mortality and XGBoost leads AKI in every regime, while fine-tuned SSL leads hospital mortality narrowly and LOS. Among the SSL objectives, MAE has the highest mean AUPRC in all nine frozen cells under both probes and in 28 of 30 reduced-label fine-tuning classification cells, while LOS differences are small once the probe loss matches the metric. Generalization to unseen databases remains untested. Future work will add an out-of-domain protocol, make converged and loss-matched probes framework protocols, and test the objectives on recurrent encoders such as GRU-D, on other input representations, and under matched compute.
